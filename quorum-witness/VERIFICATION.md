## Boundaries and unresolved physical work

The passive carrier topology and low-level NUCLEO implementation reuse existing jayis1 repository work. The device's distinct functionality is the three-voter majority/output check and per-voter dissent attribution. No claim of worldwide novelty is made.

Manufacturer PDF downloads for UM1724 and RM0368 were attempted but failed with HTTP/2 errors and HTTP/1.1 timeouts. No fresh manufacturer-document validation is claimed for this run. The cited manuals remain mandatory references for checking the exact board revision before assembly; inherited pin/register definitions were inspected for internal consistency.

No board was fabricated, populated, flashed, or connected to a physical target. Power consumption, input margins, input loading, clock accuracy, sampling latency, short-pulse visibility, watchdog operation, back-power behavior, ESD, EMC, thermal performance, regulatory compliance, and mechanical retention remain unverified. The board has no galvanic isolation or abuse-resistant input protection. Software and CAD checks do not establish physical safety or manufacturing readiness.

The data used by tests is synthetic and generated by the shipped C encoder. CRC is not authentication. Device resets with lost boot frames can evade the timestamp heuristic. A gap splits observed episodes, so counters are not necessarily counts of distinct physical incidents. LIVE reports recent compatible transport, not trustworthy voting, complete evidence, or security of the target.
